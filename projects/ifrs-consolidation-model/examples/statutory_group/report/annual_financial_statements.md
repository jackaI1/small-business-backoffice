# Notes to the consolidated financial statements

All entities and disclosures are synthetic. Amounts are CAD unless labelled otherwise. These annual statements are prepared for review, not filed or audited.

## Basis and material policies

This synthetic statement set is prepared on the IFRS Accounting Standards basis incorporated in Part I of the CPA Canada Handbook for this supported service-group scenario. The group is a continuing IFRS reporter, not a first-time adopter. Accrual accounting, historical cost and consistent policies apply. Subsidiaries are consolidated from formation; investments and all intragroup balances, income, expenses and cash transfers are eliminated. Assets and liabilities use closing FX, income and expense average FX, capital historical FX and opening retained earnings the prior translated amount. Translation OCI is reclassifiable on disposal; there are no disposals or tax effects on OCI in this scenario. Cash means unrestricted demand deposits; there are no overdrafts or cash equivalents. Operating cash flow uses the direct method, with tax paid separately. No investing, financing or non-cash transactions occurred. There are no discontinued operations, NCI, other OCI items or exceptional items. Basic/diluted EPS and segment reporting are not required for this privately held, unlisted profile.
